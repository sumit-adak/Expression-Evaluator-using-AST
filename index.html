Algorithm: Left Subtree → Right Subtree → Root.

BNF Grammar, Precedence & Reference

        
      

      

        

          
Context-Free Grammar (BNF)

          

statement  → IDENT '=' expr | expr
expr       → term (('+' | '-') term)*
term       → unary (('*' | '/' | '%') unary)*
unary      → ('-' | '+') unary | factor
factor     → primary ('^' unary)?           /* right-associative power */
primary    → NUMBER
            | IDENT
            | IDENT '(' [expr (',' expr)*] ')'
            | '(' expr ')'

Supported Functions & Constants

          

            Constants: pi (≈ 3.14159), e (≈ 2.71828)

            Trigonometry: sin(x), cos(x), tan(x) (arguments in radians)

            Arithmetic & Roots: sqrt(x), abs(x), pow(base, exp)

            Logarithms: log(x) (base 10), ln(x) (natural logarithm base e), exp(x) (ex)

            Rounding: floor(x), ceil(x), round(x)

            Multi-argument: min(a, b, ...), max(a, b, ...)
